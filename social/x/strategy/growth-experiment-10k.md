# Proposed 10k growth experiment

Status: proposal, not an approved replacement for the current operating mix.

## Audience clarification and proposed 10k path — September 8

User clarification: national coverage is intentional. The audience enjoys watching crime footage and exposed scams; the account is not primarily a neighborhood news service. CCTV, related news, scams and politics can coexist. The user is considering neutral/right-leaning politics and dropping memes. Do not assume a local pivot, a party endorsement or a fixed ideological line. Latest request asks for a path to 10,000 followers, with no new deadline; retain 1,000 by September 30 as the existing milestone.

Strategist recommendation for a 14-day test (proposal, not yet an accepted replacement of the operating mix): 60% crime/CCTV and meaningful case updates, 30% exposed scams or footage illustrating a scam, 10% relevant politics; no standalone meme allocation. At three posts daily, this is approximately 25/13/4 across 42 posts. Keep national scope, distinguish factual captions from opinion, and cover political stories when they have a concrete security/fraud/accountability connection. Neutral reporting and right-leaning commentary are distinct editorial choices; do not portray partisan commentary as neutral.

Keep the approved morning batch and three posting slots. The growth hypothesis is a repeatable sequence of compelling footage, useful explanation and follow-up that converts viewers into followers. No evidence yet proves that CCTV converts better on this account. Prioritize footage that is clear without a lengthy setup, accurate short captions, source/date, preserved context and an actual native attachment. Avoid generic memes and political filler. Establish follower baseline before forecasting.

Recommended measurement: record post category, impressions and attributable follows/profile visits where available at 24h and 72h; calculate follows per 1,000 impressions when attribution exists. Otherwise track daily account-level net followers without assigning them to individual posts. Review after 14 days using several posts per category rather than a single viral outlier. Keep sourcing and quality sustainable before increasing volume. A static bio/pinned introduction should clearly promise crimes caught on camera, scams explained and case outcomes; proposed only, no profile edit authorized here.

Distribution proposal: 15–20 minutes daily for useful contextual replies and credited interaction with relevant reporters/creators, not repeated promotional replies. User/approved actions only; this does not authorize sending replies automatically. Separate 1k→3k→10k milestones; no guaranteed date. Illustrative conversion math only: one follow per 1,000 impressions implies 10 million impressions for 10k follows; five implies 2 million. These are scenarios, not observed rates or forecasts.

Sources reviewed: https://business.x.com/en/basics/organic-best-practices and https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/recommendations . X recommends concise conversational copy and captioned video; content allowed on-platform is not necessarily eligible for recommendations. Exact mix and timetable above are strategic hypotheses, not platform-proven formulas.

## Additional early-growth tactics — proposed September 8

Priority order beyond regular posting/replies:

1. Improve profile conversion: clear bio and pinned introduction explaining the recurring value, supported by a representative inspected clip. Suggested bio: “Flagrantes de crimes, golpes expostos e os desdobramentos que importam. Brasil inteiro. Fonte e contexto em cada post.” Profile edits remain proposals, not executed.
2. Build real content collaborations with a small shortlist of relevant local reporters, footage owners and scam educators: jointly verify/explain a case, credit contributors and let each participant independently choose whether to share. Do not arrange guaranteed likes/repost/follow exchanges. No outreach sent or authorized by this discussion.
3. Invite genuinely interested existing contacts to view the page, using the user's own relevant channels and group rules; no mass DMs. A first audience should be interested viewers, not arbitrary follower counts.
4. Establish a submission route for original footage with source, incident date/location and permission to use. Verify submissions before publishing. This is a future sourcing advantage, not an already operational inbox/form.
5. Test one suitable existing X Community before creating a new community. Read its rules and contribute on-topic; do not drop the same promotion across groups.
6. Optional later cross-platform discovery using adapted short clips and a clear X destination. Add another platform only after the existing daily sourcing/review workflow is reliable; do not assume viewers will migrate.

Measure profile visits→follows where available and record partner/referral evidence rather than crediting all follower changes to a tactic. Start with profile clarity plus a shortlist of five potential content collaborators. Defer paid promotion until organic content demonstrates conversion. Avoid bought followers, follow churn and engagement-exchange groups. These are recommendations, not an automatic expansion of account access, messaging or publishing authority.
