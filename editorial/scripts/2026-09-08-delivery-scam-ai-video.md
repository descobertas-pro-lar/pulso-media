Explain the scam through a fictional scene, not a government announcement. No real victim or purported CCTV evidence.

Persistent, readable label throughout: **SIMULAÇÃO COM IA**. Bottom-only Pulso navy bar with @PulsoBrasil in off-white and green/yellow accents, as requested in this task; keep source imagery and the phone legible. No action circle because the screen is the focal point.

## Generation and editing brief

Generate separate short shots of the same adult and phone in the same apartment, preserving wardrobe, lighting and hand continuity. Natural daylight, realistic everyday setting, simple camera movement, no police imagery or dramatic crime reenactment. Generate phone screens as clean blank surfaces for editorial compositing. Use light room ambience and a single notification sound, with restrained tension underneath; no sirens or triumphant money-transfer effects. Composite the fictional UI, Portuguese text, simulation label, source credit and Pulso footer in editing. Keep the entire narration intelligible on phone speakers. Adjust shot holds to the recorded voiceover rather than speeding it up unnaturally.
