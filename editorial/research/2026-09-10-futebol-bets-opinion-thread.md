## Verification

- Brazil's July 2026 rules require risk warnings in gambling advertising and restrict advertising that can induce a bet in editorial contexts or portray betting as success: https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/julho/ministerio-da-fazenda-amplia-exigencias-de-publicidade-de-apostas-no-pais and https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/politica/noticia/2026-07/comecam-vigorar-hoje-regras-que-exigem-alertas-em-anuncios-de-bets
